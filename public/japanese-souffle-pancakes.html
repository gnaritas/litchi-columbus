The signature

Japanese soufflé pancakes —
the first Columbus has ever had

You’ve seen them on Instagram and TikTok: towering, pillowy, inches thick, and seductively jiggly. Now they’re on High Street.

What makes a soufflé pancake a soufflé pancake

Ordinary pancakes start with batter. A Japanese soufflé pancake starts with a meringue — egg whites whipped until they hold a peak — folded in gently, then cooked low and slow under a lid so the heat rises around it instead of scorching the base.

The result stands inches taller than the flap stack most of Columbus grew up on: weightless, barely sweet, and yes, it jiggles on the plate.

Litchi is the first restaurant in Columbus to offer Japanese-style soufflé pancakes. Order them at brunch or after dinner — the room pours cocktails either way.
