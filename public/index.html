The pancake that brought Columbus

First in the city: soufflé pancakes, done the Japanese way

A soufflé pancake is not a stack of flaps. It is built on a meringue of whipped egg whites, griddled low and slow until it stands inches tall and jiggles when the plate lands. The ones all of Instagram has been passing around? You can order them in Columbus now.
