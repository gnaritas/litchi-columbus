Our story

A Short North table, from a team you already know

Litchi opened this November at 940 N High St — the Short North space that once held Ampersand Asian Supper Club — and the reviews arrived fast: a perfect 5.0 from early guests on Google.

The concept belongs to Daniel Chen, the Columbus restaurateur behind 698 Restaurant and The 1126. Litchi is his take on the Pan-Asian table that Short North keeps asking for: soufflé pancakes that stand inches tall, sea bass under Thai curry, short ribs over walnut chestnut purée — brunch, dinner, and cocktails under one roof.

If you’ve eaten your way through 698 or spent an evening at the 1126, you already know the kitchen’s grammar. Litchi is what it says at breakfast — and at nine.

“We loved our experience — the first restaurant in Columbus to serve soufflé pancakes, which were delicious!”

— Google review, November 2026
