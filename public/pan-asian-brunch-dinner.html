Pan-Asian plates, brunch through last call

One menu, two shifts: everything from benedict to short ribs, poured with cocktails on the same table.

The kitchen at full stretch

Litchi’s concept runs the whole Asian table — brunch classics twisted through a Pan-Asian lens, then proper dinner plates: sea bass under Thai curry, tiger prawns that arrive still sizzling with wild mushrooms, prawn pasta sharpened with Sichuan peppercorn, and beef short ribs laid over walnut chestnut purée.

Brunch runs Tuesday through Sunday (Tuesday afternoons; weekends from 11), cocktails pour alongside every shift, and yes — soufflé pancakes are on at brunch and after dinner.
