Privacy

Plain words about this site

What the form does

When you send a note through the contact form, your name, phone, email, and message go straight to Litchi’s inbox so the team can answer. Nothing is sold, nothing is shared, and the site itself keeps no profiles.

Cookies & trackers

This site sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts. Maps load when you see them, under Google’s own privacy policy. Links to Resy leave this site under Resy’s policy.

The email pixel

When Litchi sends an email, it may include an invisible one-pixel image so the team knows the email was opened. It identifies the email, not you, and it never loads when you read mail without images displayed.
